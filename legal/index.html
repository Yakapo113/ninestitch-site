Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

NineStitch ("we", "us") is provided by Jacob M. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using NineStitch you agree to this policy.

Information you provide

  • Account: your name, email address and password (passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text). If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and an email address (which may be a private relay address).
  • Your home: address, property details such as year built, size, and type, and an optional home photo.
  • Equipment: the appliances and systems you add — type, brand, model number, install date, your notes, and any photos you take of nameplates or equipment.
  • Service pros (vendor accounts): business name, contact info, trades, hours, and business photos.

Information collected automatically

  • A push-notification token (if you allow notifications) so we can send maintenance reminders.
  • Counts of how often you use paid features (such as photo scans), so we can apply fair-use limits and control costs, and which pros you tap to contact.
  • Anonymous counts of taps on store links (such as Amazon or Home Depot) — which product or search, which task, and when — with nothing that links the tap to you. This helps us see which supplies are useful.
  • Crash reports that help us fix bugs. These do not include your name, email, or photos.

How we use your information

  • To build and maintain your personalized home-maintenance plan.
  • To send the reminders you ask for.
  • To connect you with local service professionals when you choose to contact them.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app.

Service providers we share data with

We use trusted third parties to run NineStitch. We share only what each needs:

  • Supabase — secure hosting, database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — a third-party AI service. To generate maintenance plans we send equipment details (type, brand, model). When you choose to scan a nameplate, we send that photo so the AI can read the label — the app asks your permission before your first scan, and you can always type details instead. For custom tasks we may send the task name and description to find a how-to video. We never send your name, address, or account email to Anthropic, and Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Google — the address you type (for address suggestions) and your city or ZIP code (to find local service businesses).
  • YouTube — task and equipment names, to find relevant how-to videos.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device (your push token and the reminder text).
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, if you use it. When you delete your account we also revoke NineStitch's access to your Apple ID.
  • Sentry — crash reporting, without your name, email, or photos.

Supply links (for example to Amazon or Home Depot) open the retailer's own site or app; we don't share your information with them. If we join their affiliate programs we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.

When you choose to contact a service professional, we share the details needed to complete that request (such as your name, the job, and contact info you provide). We do not sell your personal information.

Combined statistics. We may share combined, anonymous statistics with businesses or partners — for example, "6 homeowners in Provo contacted your business through NineStitch this month." These never include your name, contact details, or address, and never include anything about children.

Data retention & security

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls) to protect your data. Photos are stored at unlisted links that only your account knows.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Profile → "Delete my account." This removes your home, equipment, tasks, photos, and account record. Usage-cost records are kept without any link to you.

Sharing a home with your household

If you share a home, the people you add can see that home's details, equipment, tasks, chore assignments, and who completed what. Owners can see everyone's chore history and rewards. Members (13 and older) have their own login and can leave the home at any time; owners can remove members and kid profiles.

Children

NineStitch accounts are for adults. Children under 13 can't create an account. A parent or legal guardian who owns a home in NineStitch may add a kid profile so a child can see and check off chores. Before adding one, the parent confirms they are the child's parent or guardian and agrees to the child using NineStitch in their household.

What we store for a kid profile: the first name or nickname the parent enters, the chores assigned to the child, when each chore was done, and any rewards the parent set up (for example "$2" or "30 min screen time"). If the parent links the child's own phone with a one-time code, that phone signs in without an email, password, or other contact details, and we store a push-notification token so it can receive reminders about the child's own chores.

What we don't do: we don't ask a child for their email, phone number, address, photos, location, or any other contact information. Kid mode doesn't use photo scans, AI features, local-pro search, store links, or videos, so nothing about a child is sent to Anthropic, Google, YouTube, or retailers. A child's information is never sold, used for advertising, or shared outside the household, except with the service providers that run the app (Supabase for hosting, Expo to deliver the child's reminders).

How long we keep a child's information: only as long as the parent keeps the kid profile. A kid profile (name, chores, rewards) stays until the parent removes it or deletes the home or their account, and is then deleted right away. A child's phone login and push token are deleted as soon as the phone is unlinked, the kid profile is removed, or the home is deleted; a phone that starts setup but never finishes is deleted within a day. Past chore completions stay in the home's history without the child's name. Deleted information may remain in routine backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

Parents' choices: a parent can review, rename, or remove a kid profile at any time in Profile → People & sharing. Removing a kid profile deletes the child's name, rewards, and the link to their phone; past chore completions stay in the home's history without the child's name. Deleting the parent's home or account removes all of its kid profiles. Parents can also contact us at support@ninestitch.app to review or delete their child's information, or to stop any further collection.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your home, equipment, and tasks in the app at any time.
  • You can turn reminders on or off in Profile.
  • You can request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.
