NineStitch.

Home maintenance, handled.

NineStitch helps homeowners treat their house like an asset — a personalized maintenance plan, reminders before things break, step-by-step guides, and a line to local pros when a job is bigger than a weekend.

  • A plan built for your home, appliances, and climate.
  • Friendly reminders before each task is due — you choose when.
  • How-to guides and videos matched to your equipment.
  • A home-health score so you know you're on track at a glance.

The name comes from the old proverb “a stitch in time saves nine.” A small fix now prevents a big repair later — that's the whole idea.
